Geoduck support

Geoduck is a travel map: log the countries and cities you've been to, add your photos and trips, and share them with the people you choose.

Contact us

Email app.geoduck@gmail.com with any question, problem or idea. We aim to reply within 2 working days.

Report someone or something

In the app, open the person's profile, post or comment, tap More options (…) and choose Report. You can also block anyone from the same menu. We review reports and aim to act within 24 hours. For anything urgent, email app.geoduck@gmail.com.
