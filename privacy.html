Geoduck privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Geoduck is a travel log: you mark the countries and cities you've been to, add your photos and trips, and choose who else can see your map. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Geoduck is run by Darshan Ravujee ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email us at app.geoduck@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. If you use Apple or Google, they share your email address and an account identifier with us. Google may also share your name and profile picture link; these are stored with your login record but Geoduck doesn't show or use them.
  • Username and display name that you choose when you set up your profile.
  • Your age confirmation. You confirm that you're 13 or older. We don't ask for your date of birth.
  • Your privacy setting (private or public), and your friends, followers, follow requests and the people you've blocked.

Your travels

  • Places: the countries, regions and cities you log, and optionally when you first went there and whether you lived there.
  • Trips: their titles, dates, stops, the routes between stops, and whether a trip is hidden.
  • Photos and videos you add, and their date and location. Geoduck reads the date and GPS location stored in a photo (or in your phone's photo library) to place it in the city where it was taken and to suggest trips. We store that date and location with the photo. Photos are uploaded as a copy resized to at most 2048 pixels on the longest side. Videos up to 20 MB are uploaded as the original file; bigger ones stay only on your phone. Small preview images (thumbnails) are uploaded too.
  • A few app settings that follow you between phones: which photo is each city's cover, and whether trip routes show on the map.

Likes and comments you leave on posts, and your activity list: who liked or commented on your posts, who commented after you on a post, and who asked to be your friend or follow you.

Reports. If you report someone, we keep who made the report, who it's about, the reason you chose and when you sent it.

What we don't collect. We don't track your location in the background: the only locations we have are the ones stored in the photos you choose to add and the places you log yourself. We don't read your contacts. The app can only see the photos you pick or allow it to access.

Why we collect it

We use your data only to run Geoduck:

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to back up your places, trips, photos and videos and keep them in sync across your phones;
  • to show your map and trips to the people you've allowed to see them;
  • to review reports and keep the service safe, including automatically refusing usernames, names, captions and comments that contain slurs or abusive words.

We don't show ads. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We don't use third-party tracking or analytics tools.

Where it's stored

Your account, travels, photos and videos are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in Singapore. Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Photo and video files are kept in private storage that only your own account can read. Supabase keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

Your phone also keeps its own copy, so the app works offline. If you make a backup file in Settings, it's saved wherever you choose to put it; we never see it.

Other services the app talks to

Some features need to contact other services. They receive your phone's IP address and the request itself, but not your account details:

  • OpenFreeMap supplies the map images.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) supplies city outlines: the app sends a city's name and country.
  • Route servers (the public OSRM servers, including one run by FOSSGIS) work out road and walking routes between a trip's stops: the app sends the coordinates of the stops' cities and, so the route follows the roads you actually took, the locations of some of your photos taken along the way.
  • Expo delivers app updates: the app checks with Expo's servers for a newer version.
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them.

Who can see what

  • You can see everything in your account.
  • Private account (the default). Only your friends and the followers you've approved can see your map (places) and your trips.
  • Public account. Anyone signed in to Geoduck can see your map and your trips.
  • Visible to every signed-in user, whatever your setting: your username, your display name, whether your account is private, and how many followers, people you follow and friends you have. People can find you by searching for your username or name.
  • Hidden trips. A trip marked "Hide from others" is seen only by you, and the cities on it stay off other people's view of your map.
  • Photos and videos are seen only by you, unless you put a photo in a post.
  • Posts are seen by the audience you pick for each one: only you, your friends, or (on a public account) anyone who can see your profile. A post holds a smaller copy of each photo you choose, without the location data stored inside the photo file. If you leave "Show location" on, the post names the city. Deleting a post removes those copies.
  • Likes and comments on a post are seen by everyone who can see the post. If its audience narrows, they narrow with it. People you've blocked, and people who blocked you, never see your likes and comments, and you never see theirs.
  • Your activity list is seen only by you. Its entries stop being shown after 90 days, and are deleted from our servers the next time you open Activity (and when you delete your account).
  • Blocking. When you block someone, any friendship or follows between you end, neither of you can find or see the other, and the likes and comments either of you left on the other's posts are removed.
  • Us. We can access stored data when we need to run or fix the service, or to review a report.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete a place, trip, photo or video, it's removed from our servers; we keep only an empty marker so your other phones know to delete it too.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Stats → Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, profile, places, trips, photos, videos, posts, likes, comments, activity, connections and blocks, and reports made by you or about you, from our servers, and removes Geoduck's copy from your phone. Photos stay in your phone's own photo library. It can't be undone.

If you can't use the app, email app.geoduck@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app, download it as a backup file (Stats → Settings), and delete it at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to object to how we use it. Email app.geoduck@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission).

Children

Geoduck is for people aged 13 and over. You must confirm you're 13 or older to create a profile. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete it. If you think a child under 13 is using Geoduck, email app.geoduck@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used or who can see it, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
