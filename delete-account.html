Delete your Geoduck account

In the app

Open Geoduck and go to Stats → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. This permanently erases your account, profile, places, trips, photos, videos, posts, likes, comments, activity, connections and blocks from our servers, and removes Geoduck's copy from your phone. Photos stay in your phone's own photo library. It can't be undone.

If you can't use the app

Email app.geoduck@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it and everything above within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

Nothing from your account. Our hosting provider's standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) are deleted automatically on their usual schedule.
