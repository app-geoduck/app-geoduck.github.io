Only add content you have the right to share.

3. What's not allowed

  • break, overload, or get around the security of the service, or access accounts or data that aren't yours.

This applies to everything others can see, including your username, display name and trip titles. There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users: Geoduck automatically refuses words used to abuse people, and we remove content and accounts that break these rules.

4. Reporting, blocking and enforcement

If someone breaks these rules, report them from their profile (More options (…) → Report), or report a post or a comment from its More options (…) menu. You can also block anyone: they won't be able to find you or see your travels, you'll stop following each other, and the likes and comments either of you left on the other's posts are removed.

We review reports and aim to act within 24 hours. We may remove content, and suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, without warning if the problem is serious.
